## Summary

Configure Entra ID diagnostic settings to route identity logs to a central destination, such as a Log Analytics workspace[^1]. In diagnostic settings, include AuditLogs and SigninLogs to capture MFA-related events[^1]. Use Log Analytics queries to analyze SigninLogs and AuditLogs for MFA activity[^2].

## Design Decision

> [!NOTE] Route all Entra ID diagnostic logs (SigninLogs, AuditLogs, ProvisioningLogs) to a Log Analytics workspace (or other designated sink) to ensure centralized storage, retention, and analysis of MFA-related events.

## Prerequisites

* **Licensing:** The required licenses vary based on the monitoring and health capability. A working Microsoft Entra tenant with a Microsoft Entra ID P1 or P2 license associated with it. [^2]
* **Permissions/Roles:** Appropriate roles for Azure Monitor and Microsoft Entra ID. [^2]
* **Dependencies:** A Log Analytics workspace and access to that workspace. [^2]

## Implementation Steps

### Configure Entra ID diagnostic settings to route logs to Log Analytics

1. Sign in to the Microsoft Entra admin center as at least a Security Administrator. [^1]
2. Browse to Entra ID > Monitoring & health > Diagnostic settings. [^1]
3. Select Add diagnostic setting. [^1]
4. On the Diagnostic setting page, provide a name for the diagnostic setting. [^1]
5. Under Logs, select AuditLogs and SigninLogs. [^1]
6. Under Destination details, select Send to Log Analytics, and then select your new log analytics workspace. [^1]
7. Select Save. [^1]
8. Your selected logs might take up to 15 minutes for the logs to populate in your Log Analytics workspace. [^1]
