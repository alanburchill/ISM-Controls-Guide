## Summary

Monitor the Microsoft 365 Admin Portal Service Health to monitor for any service vulnerabilities announcements.[^1] The Service Health page displays current health, active incidents, advisories, and issues requiring action for your tenant, with customizable views and email alerts.[^1] A vulnerability scanner is used at least daily to identify missing patches or updates for vulnerabilities in online services.[^2]

## Design Decision

> [!NOTE] Use the Service Health page in the Microsoft 365 Admin Center as the primary source for monitoring service vulnerability announcements. Configure alerts to notify security and IT teams when new incidents or advisories appear.

## Implementation Steps

### Service Health Monitoring in Microsoft 365 Admin Center

1. Sign in to the Microsoft 365 admin center at https://admin.microsoft.com with an admin account. [^1]

2. In the left-hand navigation, go to **Health** > **Service health**, or select the **Service health** card on the **Home** dashboard. [^1]

3. On the **Service health** page, view the health state of each cloud service. The **Overview** tab shows active incidents and advisories. [^1]

4. To view details of a posted service health issue, select the issue title to open the details pane. The details include: Title, ID, Last updated, Affected services, Issue type, Status, User impact, and All Updates. [^1]

5. To customize your view of service health, select **Customize** > **Custom view**, and clear the checkboxes for services you want to filter out. [^1]

6. To receive email notifications of new incidents or status changes, select **Customize** > **Email**, choose “Send me email notifications about service health,” and specify up to two email addresses, whether to be notified about incidents or advisories, and the services. [^1]

7. If you don’t see a known issue, select **Report an issue** and complete the short form to notify Microsoft. [^1]

8. Optional: Use the Microsoft 365 Admin app on your mobile device to view Service health and receive push notifications. [^1]
