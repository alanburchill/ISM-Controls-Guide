## Summary

Block Office applications from creating executable content by enabling the ASR rule for Office apps/macros via Intune, reducing the risk of Office macros or documents delivering executable payloads.[^8][^5]

Not provided in source documentation.

## Design Decision

> [!NOTE]
> The policy **Block Office applications from creating executable content** will be deployed via **Intune Attack Surface Reduction rules**. Office apps from creating executable content will be blocked.

## Prerequisites

- Permissions/Roles
  - Access to the **Intune** console to create and deploy the ASR policy that blocks Office apps from creating executable content. [^2]

- Dependencies
  - **Microsoft Intune** managed devices and enrollment in **Entra ID** or hybrid Azure AD joined to receive policy. [^2]
  - **Microsoft Defender Antivirus** installed and active on devices to support ASR enforcement. [^3]

- Testing and Validation
  - ASR rules should be tested in audit mode before enforcement to identify compatibility issues. [^1]

## Implementation Steps

### Enable ASR rule to block Office apps from creating executable content via Intune

1. In the Intune ASR policy, locate the setting **Block Office applications from creating executable content** (Office apps/macros creating executable content).[^8]
2. Set the value to **Block**.[^5]
3. Validate by testing the ASR policy in audit mode before enforcement to ensure compatibility in your environment.[^1]

> [!NOTE]
> ASR rules should be tested for compatibility issues in any environment before enforcement.[^1]
