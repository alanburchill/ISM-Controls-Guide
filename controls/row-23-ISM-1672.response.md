## Summary

Configure **Microsoft Office macros antivirus scanning** via **Intune** and import the **ACSC Office Hardening Policy** via **Intune**.[^1]

[^1]: [Essential Eight configure Microsoft Office macro settings - Essential Eight](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/compliance/anz/e8-macro)

## Design Decision

Use **Microsoft Intune** to (1) configure **Scan macros in Office files** and (2) import the **ACSC Office Hardening Policy** for deployment.

> [!NOTE]
> Centralize Office macro hardening by enabling **Scan macros in Office files** and importing the **ACSC Office Hardening Policy** via **Microsoft Intune**.

## Prerequisites

**Permissions/Roles:**

- Access to the **Microsoft Intune admin center** to configure and deploy policies, including importing the **ACSC Office Hardening policy** files and assigning policies to groups.[^1][^3]
- Ability to import policies into **Intune** and reference local policy files during import.[^1]
- Ability to manage **Trusted Publishers** certificates in **Intune** (to allow macros signed by trusted publishers).[^1]
- Ability to assign imported policies to groups (e.g., All Office Users; Allow macro execution - Trusted Publisher).[^1]

**Dependencies:**

- Availability of the **ACSC Office Hardening policy** files for import into **Intune**.[^1]
- Availability of the **Macros Enabled for Trusted Publishers** and **All Macros Disabled** policy files for import into **Intune**.[^1]
- **Windows Defender** must be running on devices to support macro antivirus scanning.[^1]
- Defender for Endpoint integration with **Intune** for logging and monitoring macro executions; ensure devices are onboarded to **MDE**.[^1]
- Capability to deploy policies via **Settings Catalog** or **Administrative Templates** in **Intune**.[^3]
- External macro hardening guidance informs the design (e.g., ASD Blueprint).[^2]
