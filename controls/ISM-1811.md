## Design Decision

Use **Azure Recovery Services vault** to (1) configure **geo-redundant storage with encryption enabled** and (2) deploy **Configure-BackupVault.ps1** via **PowerShell**.

> [!NOTE]
> Deploying **Configure-BackupVault.ps1** via PowerShell ensures backups are stored in a vault with **geo-redundancy** and **encryption enabled**.
