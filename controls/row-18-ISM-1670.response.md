## Summary

Enable an Attack Surface Reduction (ASR) rule to block Adobe Reader from creating child processes, reducing the PDF application's attack surface.[^1] Configure and enforce the ASR policy via Intune to block Adobe Reader on endpoints.[^1] This approach aligns with organizational hardening of user applications and ASR configuration guidance.[^2]

[^1]: [Attack Surface Reduction policy settings for endpoint security in Intune](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/intune/intune-service/protect/endpoint-security-asr-profile-settings#attack-surface-reduction-mdm#attack-surface-reduction-policy-settings-for-endpoint-security-in-intune)
[^2]: [System hardening - User applications](https://blueprint.asd.gov.au/security-and-governance/system-security-plan/system-hardening-user-apps/)
